# Active entry mode: sweep-reclaim-v9

User authorized activation on 3 November 2026, Thailand. This is an experimental rule change, not a claim that the six-case exploratory study proved profitability.

The main 15m scanner continues discovering all active Binance crypto USDT perpetuals locally (Kraken subset in the cloud). Its structure/regime/ATR eligibility gates now produce up to five watch seeds per discovery round. It no longer sends direct v8 entries. Seeds are created only from newly observed setups after activation; previously sent alerts are not converted into new entries. The seed level is the hypothetical structural Stop from that setup, not measured stop concentration of other traders.

The minute watcher waits up to four hours for a price excursion beyond that level. After the first excursion, it requires two consecutive completed 1m closes back on the intended side within 15 minutes and before seed expiry. Missing minutes break the consecutive-close streak. The original partial seed minute is excluded. Confirmation expires after 90 seconds; breaking the new Stop or closing back on the wrong side before entry invalidates it. Entry uses a current executable-side quote, not a historical next-minute open. Stop is beyond the observed sweep extreme plus 0.15 ATR known when the seed was registered. It is never widened after delivery.

At confirmation, fetch current 15m context for the coin and BTC. Reject opposing BTC trend, inferior directional 30m strength, missing/stale context, lost reclaim, chase beyond 0.3% or 0.2R of the confirmation reference, risk outside 0.3–3%, spread above 0.1% or above 10% of risk. AI approval remains mandatory. Recheck price after AI; reject changes above 0.1R and expired/invalid entries. No model can guarantee a return or reveal market-wide Stops.

A separate private Telegram document owns sweep watch/done/journal state. An atomic caption lock grants one owner per minute and all network writes have a deadline before the next minute. Attempt state is persisted before send; ambiguous delivery is never retried. All providers share a minimum 15-minute gap between delivery attempts. Discovery writes the primary state only; the minute watcher never overwrites it. New private data is kept in .sweep/ and excluded from public source.

Local Windows scheduled watcher runs each minute, aligned to the minute boundary, through the existing restartable scheduled task. It replaces the old early-microbase observation worker. GitHub backup checks every five minutes, may be delayed or miss short confirmations, and uses Kraken prices. It yields while local discovery was recently active. This is best-effort free scheduling, not guaranteed 24/7 minute-level coverage when the PC is off. Incomplete history and stale confirmations never produce catch-up alerts.

Delivered v9 alerts are imported into local outcome tracking from the private sweep-state mirror. Entry training is segregated into entry-model-v9.json, continues only in shadow and includes the eight contextual features. v8 data/models remain separate. The earlier A/B/C comparison is a discovery eligibility study, not the live entry strategy or delivered-alert cohort after this change.

Validation includes two-close causality, both directions, missing bars, expired/invalidated confirmations, current BTC/quote guards, price movement after AI, AI rejection, cross-run minute ownership, persist-before-delivery, ambiguous delivery and the global gap. Profitability remains unproven and must be judged on new delivered outcomes including execution costs.
