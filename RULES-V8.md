# Live rule change: structure-regime-atr-v8

Activated on user instruction on 3 November 2026 (Thailand). These thresholds are preregistered experimental choices, not empirically proven improvements. Both local Binance and cloud Kraken runners use the same gate logic, with BTCUSDT and PF_XBTUSD benchmarks respectively. Missing benchmark/history rejects the candidate and is counted explicitly. The existing scan schedule, fresh closed 15m breakout/retest, quote guards, AI approval and maximum one delivery attempt per round remain.

- Entry: no more than 0.5 ATR14 beyond the original 20-bar breakout level, measured from the executable-side quote. Keep the existing 0.3% maximum drift from the just-closed retest reference and 0.1% maximum spread.
- Regime: BTC EMA9/21 and one-hour EMA21 slope define up/down/neutral. Reject the opposite regime; neutral may pass. For altcoins also require the directional 30m return at least as strong as BTC. BTC itself is exempt from comparison to itself.
- Stop: beyond the retest extreme by at least the larger of the original 0.1% buffer and 0.15 ATR14; never move it inside the old structure Stop. New risk must remain 0.3–3% of reference entry and spread must be at most 10% of Stop distance. Recompute 2R/3R/4R from this Stop. Stops are fixed in outcome evaluation; no retrospective widening.
- Confidence text: label the existing heuristic as structural quality, not a chance of winning.
- ML: disable live ML evidence from the underperforming old cohort. Keep Groq/Gemini numerical setup review required. Segregate v8 fitting into entry-model-v8.json and use eight features: previous four plus base distance/ATR, directional return relative to BTC, spread/risk, and risk/ATR. Log predictions before outcomes only in shadow. No automatic promotion. Purge four-hour label overlap from primary temporal holdout training.

Prospective paper comparison logs every quote-reviewed candidate, including rejected candidates, to private state and local .ml/challenger-observations.jsonl. Variants: baseline old guards/Stop; A adds distance; B adds regime/relative strength; C adds ATR Stop/cost checks and is live. The eligibility variants are compared before LLM approval, so their cohort must not be described as delivered Telegram alerts. Primary delivered-alert training remains separate. The trainer updates outcomes every two hours, up to 40 newly attempted variant outcomes per run. TP/SL ordering uses 1m bars and aggTrades for ambiguous or partial minutes. Timeout is marked at the last fully closed 1m close within four hours. Report gross reference R, actual available funding rates, and assumed combined fees/slippage scenarios 5/10/20 bps per round trip, plus reference drawdown at 10 bps. These are not actual account PnL; position overlap and actual execution differ.

Review checkpoint: at least 100 priced outcomes and seven Thai market dates per variant. This is a checkpoint, not proof. Examine out-of-sample net results, uncertainty and market/time concentration before any later ML promotion or additional thresholds. Existing rules are not automatically restored merely because no entry passes. Private observations, outcomes, model weights and credentials must never be published to the public repository.
